In each paper, briefly to look for:

Key Theme and Trend: 
	Group literature by topics, themes, or chronological developments to identify patterns.

Research Gaps and Limitations: 
	Identify unexplored areas, conflicting findings, or methodological weaknesses in paper.

Methodological Approach: 
	How the research was conducted (e.g., qualitative, quantitative, specific techniques) to identify best practices or gaps in methods.

Theoretical Frameworks: 
	Identify the core theories, models, or definitions foundational to the paper.

Significant Findings/Conclusions: 
	Record the main results of seminal and current studies, highlighting consensus and debates.
